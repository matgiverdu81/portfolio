About

I've been making pictures professionally for eleven years, and unprofessionally for a lot longer than that.

I trained as a photojournalist before moving into portrait and wedding work, and it still shows — I'd rather wait for a real moment than build one. Most sessions start with a walk and very little direction. Based on the coast, available for travel most of the year.

Prints are made in-house on archival paper. Film is developed and scanned by a lab I've worked with since 2016.
